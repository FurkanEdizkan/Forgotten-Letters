# Carcass Front — the book, bound

Scope: every surface (live map, players, rosters, zones and lore, chronicle, all admin). Mode: Experience for the live map (TV/projector and phones at the table); Read for standings, rosters, lore and chronicle; Operate for admin. User-pinned: follow the Carcass Front book; both registers by surface; white book paper replaces cream parchment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is the Carcass Front book, bound: painted night spreads for the live campaign, rulebook pages for everything you read or fill in. It refuses the generic cream-parchment fantasy app and the dark dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Night register — olive-black smoke (#15130e), bone text, ember accents, the map as the lit plate. Page register — bright book white, black Garamond, red blackletter section heads, bold small-caps subheads over a red rule, grey-wash rules boxes with a red hairline border, grey-header stat tables, blackletter running footers on a long hairline. Title caps in Pirata One, tracked wide, like the book's chapter titles. Red cross ✠ device as authored SVG.

STORY: A player glances at the TV and sees a war, not an app; opens their phone and reads their warband like a page of the book; the CM fills in the ledger like marking the book's own tracker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Live map full-bleed in smoke; top-left the cover lockup (TRENCH ✠ CRUSADE over CARCASS FRONT); battles as a strip of rules-box tabs; tapping a zone slides in a book page from the right, with running footer.

FORM: Pinned by the user (the Carcass Front book, both registers); no roll — brief-pinned direction beats the roll. Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
