# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Trench Crusade tabletop wargame hobbyists — players and GMs who create, share, and discover scenarios and campaigns for Trench Crusade (expanding to other game systems later). They use the product both at a desk while building content (map editor, scenario/campaign forms) and mid-game-prep, referencing rules, official content, and community scenarios. Primary users skew toward content creators/GMs building scenarios, with casual browsers discovering and reacting (voting, favoriting, commenting) as a secondary but important audience.

## Product Purpose

A community-driven scenario repository for wargame scenario generation and sharing. Users create campaigns and scenarios (story, event tables, victory conditions, a 2D canvas map editor), publish them, and the community browses, filters, votes, favorites, and comments on them. Official CMS-managed content (rules, official scenarios) sits alongside user-generated content. Success looks like an active flow of creators publishing quality scenarios and a community that reliably discovers, engages with, and returns to them.

## Brand Personality

Gritty & tactical. Dark, precise, war-room feel that evokes Trench Crusade's grimdark WWI-occult tone — closer to a tactical planning tool or military ops dashboard than a hobby site: dense, confident, no-nonsense. Voice is direct and competent, not playful or cutesy.

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid the cream/sand AI-SaaS look, generic card grids, gradient-hero templates, and anything that reads as a theme-agnostic startup product bolted onto this content. The UI should feel like a real tool built specifically for this game and its tone, not a template.

## Design Principles

- **Confidence over cuteness.** The map editor and CRUD flows should feel like precise tactical software, not a toy or a generic SaaS form.
- **Respect the source material's tone.** Grimdark WWI-occult atmosphere informs mood and palette without becoming decorative pastiche layered on top of a generic layout.
- **Creators over browsers.** Optimize the primary flows (map editor, scenario/campaign creation and editing) for confident, low-friction work; discovery/browse should feel like uncovering real intel or archives, not a generic content feed.
- **Legible under pressure.** Dense tactical data (event tables, map data, filters, multi-step forms) must stay scannable — when atmosphere and clarity conflict, clarity wins.
- **Community trust, not engagement bait.** Votes, favorites, comments, and public profiles should feel earned and transparent rather than gamified for retention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: solid color contrast (including on a dark, tactical palette), full keyboard navigation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support throughout. No additional formal requirements beyond AA at this stage.
