## What is Forgotten Letters?

A community archive for wargame scenarios and campaigns, starting with Trench Crusade.
You can write scenarios, build campaign graphs, share warbands, and browse what others
have published.

## Is this official?

No. Forgotten Letters is a fan-made, community-run archive. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or connected to any game publisher. Trench Crusade and every other game
system referenced here remain the property of their respective publishers.

## Do you host the official rules?

No. We host **community-authored** content only. The pages under Rules are authoring
context for scenario writers — they deliberately contain no official rules text. Buy the
rules from the publisher.

## Is it free?

Yes. Creating, publishing, and reading community content is free and always will be.
The project is funded by unobtrusive ads and optional supporter tiers. Supporters get
more storage, no ads, and some convenience features — never exclusive access to
community content.

## Who owns what I publish?

You keep ownership of what you write. By publishing you grant us permission to host and
display it. You can delete your content at any time.

## Can I self-host this?

Yes. The project is open source and designed to be self-hostable — see the repository.

## How do I report content?

Use the report control on any scenario, campaign, or comment. Content that reproduces
copyrighted rules text, or that infringes a publisher's IP, is removed.
