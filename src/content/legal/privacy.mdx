## What we collect

- **Account data** — your email address, username, and password hash. The password
  itself is never stored.
- **Content** — what you publish, and files you upload.
- **Sign-in data** — if you sign in with Google or GitHub, we store the account
  identifier they give us, not your password with them.

## What we do not collect

We do not sell personal data. We do not run cross-site tracking for advertising: the
default ad provider is privacy-friendly and serves no personalized ads.

## Cookies

A session cookie keeps you signed in. If a consent-gated ad provider is enabled, no
advertising cookies are set until you consent.

## Email

We send transactional email only — verification, password reset, and security notices.

## Your rights

You can export or delete your account and content from account settings. Deleting your
account removes your profile, content, and uploaded files.

## Contact

Open an issue on the repository for privacy questions.
