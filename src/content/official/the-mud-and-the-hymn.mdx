> A community-written example scenario, published as a reference for the format. It
> uses no official rules text.

## Premise

Two warbands meet at a flooded crossing. Neither can afford to withdraw, and the ground
gives way underfoot.

## Setup

- **Players:** 2
- **Table:** 3&prime; × 3&prime;
- **Duration:** roughly 90 minutes
- **Edition:** written against `v1`

Place a channel of impassable water running across the centre of the table. Set three
crossing points along it, evenly spaced. Scatter cover along both banks.

## Deployment

Players roll off. The winner picks a bank; the other deploys opposite. Deploy within
6&Prime; of your table edge, alternating one model at a time.

## Objectives

Control crossings at the end of the game. A crossing is controlled by the player with
more models within 3&Prime; of it.

| Crossings held          | Result           |
| ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| All three               | Decisive victory |
| Two                     | Victory          |
| One each, one contested | Draw             |

## Special rules

**The ground gives.** The first time a model ends its move within 3&Prime; of a crossing,
that crossing becomes unstable. Unstable crossings are difficult terrain for the rest of
the game.

**Hymn at dusk.** From the fourth round, models more than 18&Prime; from any friendly model
cannot be targeted by ranged attacks — the light has gone.

## Aftermath

The winner takes first pick of any salvage. In a campaign, the loser's warband arrives at
the next scenario carrying one unrecovered injury.
