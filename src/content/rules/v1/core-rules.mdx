## What a scenario assumes

Every scenario published here assumes readers already own the official rules. When you
write a scenario, you can rely on the reader knowing:

- the turn sequence and how initiative is determined
- how actions are declared and resolved
- the standard measurements and terrain categories
- how injuries and post-battle recovery work

Write your scenario to **modify** those baselines, not to restate them.

## Writing special rules

A special rule is clearest when it names the baseline it changes:

| Instead of            | Write                                                             |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Models move slower." | "Models treat all terrain as difficult."                          |
| "Shooting is harder." | "Apply an additional penalty to ranged attacks beyond 12&Prime;." |
| "The fog matters."    | "Models more than 18&Prime; away cannot be targeted."             |

Ambiguity is the most common reason a scenario is hard to play. If two readers could
resolve a rule differently, the rule needs another sentence.
