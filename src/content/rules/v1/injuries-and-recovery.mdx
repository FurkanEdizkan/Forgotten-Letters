> Community reference. The official rules define the actual injury tables and results.

## Between games

Campaign scenarios usually resolve in this order:

1. Determine the winner and any scenario-specific rewards.
2. Roll for injuries on models taken out of action.
3. Apply advancement.
4. Spend rewards, repair or replace equipment.
5. Record the result and move to the next node in the campaign graph.

If your campaign changes any of these steps, note it on the campaign rather than
repeating it in every scenario.

## Recording results

The battle tracker records the outcome and the post-battle report, so a campaign's
history stays attached to the warbands that played it.
