## What to specify in a scenario

If your scenario constrains warband construction, say so explicitly and early. Readers
build before they arrive at the table.

- **Points limit** — state it, or state that the scenario is played at any agreed size.
- **Faction restrictions** — if the scenario is written for particular factions, name them.
- **Mandatory or forbidden units** — list them rather than implying them.
- **Injuries carried in** — say whether the warband arrives fresh or wounded.

## Campaign rosters

In a campaign, a warband persists between games. State how your campaign handles:

- advancement between scenarios
- permanent injuries and replacements
- captured or lost equipment
- whether a defeated warband may re-roster before the next game

The campaign graph editor stores these as campaign-level notes, so they apply across
every scenario in the branch.
