> **This is a community reference, not the rulebook.** Trench Crusade is the property of
> its publisher. Nothing here reproduces official rules text.

## What changed in this edition

This page documents the **v2** edition. Scenarios written against `v1` remain readable at
their pinned version — use the edition selector to switch.

Notable differences authors should account for:

- terrain categories were consolidated, so scenarios that named the old categories
  should be reviewed
- the post-battle sequence changed order, which affects campaign scenarios that
  interleave rewards with recovery

## What a scenario assumes

As in v1, scenarios assume the reader owns the official rules. Write to modify the
baseline rather than restate it.

## Writing special rules

Name the baseline you are changing. If two readers could resolve a rule differently,
the rule needs another sentence.
