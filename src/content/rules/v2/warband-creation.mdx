## Second-edition notes

Roster construction changed enough between editions that a scenario written for `v1`
may not balance at `v2` without review. When you publish, pin the edition you playtested
against — the scenario form records it.

## What to specify

- points limit
- faction restrictions
- mandatory or forbidden units
- whether warbands arrive fresh or carry injuries
